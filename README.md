### 🧮 The Magnum Opus: [SG Child Cost & Support Calculator](https://YOUR-APP-URL-HERE.com)
*(Repository: [`sg-parenting-costs`](https://github.com/vectorforge21/sg-parenting-costs))*

Singaporeans are routinely paralyzed by sensational tabloid headlines declaring that *"It costs $1,000,000 to raise a child in Singapore!"*—usually published by an insurer trying to sell you an investment-linked policy (ILP).

Meanwhile, government policy documents on Baby Bonus, Child Development Account (CDA) First Step, dollar-for-dollar co-matching, MediSave Maternity Grants, ECDA preschool subsidies, and MOE Edusave accounts read like bureaucratic sacred scripture decipherable only by seasoned civil servants.

**This app cuts through the noise:**
- **Gross vs. Net Out-of-Pocket:** Tracks the real out-of-pocket ledger year-by-year from gestation (0) to tertiary independence (23).
- **Statutory Subsidy Dissector:** Accurately accounts for restricted grant mechanics (cash vs. matched CDA savings vs. direct fee offset caps) instead of treating vouchers like raw liquidity.
- **Transparent Assumptions:** Every single diaper price, infant formula tin, AOP/POP preschool fee, and tuition metric is editable and backed by sourced baseline benchmarks.
- **Zero Sponsored Bias:** Whether you want to raise a stoic monk on library books and polyclinic visits, or an Olympic violin champion on private enrichment, the calculator reveals the true bottom line without judgment.
